# How it works: why your tape measure hook is loose (on purpose)

Episode 2 of "How ___ actually works". Everything here comes from patents and tape makers' own pages (sources at the bottom).

## The wobbly hook isn't broken

- The end hook rides on rivets that pass through oval slots in the blade. That's the wobble you feel.
- It slides a limited distance, **equal to the thickness of the hook itself** ([US7627958](https://patents.google.com/patent/US7627958), [US5699623](https://patents.google.com/patent/US5699623)).
- **Push** it into a corner (inside measurement) and the hook slides **in**. **Hook** it over an edge (outside measurement) and it slides **out**. The two readings start from opposite faces of the hook, and the travel makes up for its thickness, so both read true. The 1997 patent puts it this way: "so that both interior and exterior measurements are accurate".
- On [U.S. Tape](https://www.ustape.com/hidden-features-of-a-tape-measure/)'s tapes, the hook is 1/16 inch thick and the first inch is 1/16 inch short ("true zero"). That's one maker's spec, not a universal number.

## Why it should never slam

- A spiral (coil) spring winds up as you pull the blade out and stores the energy that pulls it back ([US10712142](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10712142B2/en)).
- Fast automatic retraction "often produces great impact force" ([US5699623](https://patents.google.com/patent/US5699623)), and the hook takes the hit.
- Makers warn against it. [Klein Tools](https://www.kleintools.com/product/93025) says "do not allow the blade to retract into case at full speed", and [U.S. Tape](https://www.ustape.com/properly-maintaining-your-tape-measure/) says whipping it back can damage "the inner workings, the end hook and the blade markings". **Guide the blade home with your hand.**

## Why the blade doesn't flop

The blade is steel with a **curved** (concavo-convex) cross-section. The curve makes thin steel stiffer and harder to buckle, so the blade can stick out further on its own before it folds. That distance is called "standout" ([US10712142](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10712142B2/en), [U.S. Tape](https://www.ustape.com/hidden-features-of-a-tape-measure/)).

## Two more hidden features (U.S. Tape)

- Nearly every hook has a small **slot** that grabs the head of a nail or screw, so you can measure alone.
- The **serrated edge** of the hook can scratch a mark when you run it back and forth.
